Site Plan

Brazzaville Chamber of Commerce

Site Name

Brazzaville Chamber of Commerce

Site Purpose

This website will help businesses in Brazzaville connect with each other and with the community. It will share local news, list member businesses, and explain how to join the chamber.

Target Market

Business owners, entrepreneurs, and residents of Brazzaville, Republic of the Congo.

Site Goals

  • Help people find local businesses easily
  • Get more businesses to join the chamber
  • Share news and events happening in the city

User Personas

Marie - 38 years old, owns a small clothing shop. She wants more customers.

Jean-Paul - 45 years old, works in logistics. He wants to network with other businesses.

Sarah - 29 years old, just moved back to Brazzaville. She wants to learn about local businesses and events.

Scenarios

  • A business owner visits the site to learn how to join the chamber.
  • A resident checks the site to see upcoming events in town.
  • A visitor looks at the directory to find a local restaurant.

SEO Plan

  • Use keywords like "Brazzaville business" in the page content
  • Add the site to Google Business
  • Ask member businesses to link back to the site
