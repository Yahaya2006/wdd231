How a Slam Competition Works

A poetry slam is a competitive event in which poets perform their original work and are scored by judges selected from the audience. Here are the fundamental rules:

The Scoring System

Slam poetry uses a scoring system inspired by Olympic gymnastics. Five judges are selected randomly from the audience at the beginning of the event.

Five Judges

Five audience members are selected as judges at random before the competition begins.

Score from 0 to 10

Each judge gives a score between 0 and 10, including decimals. 10 is a perfect score.

Drop High and Low

The highest and lowest scores are dropped to reduce bias. The three remaining scores are added.

Maximum Score: 30

The maximum possible score for a single poem is 30 points (3 × 10).
